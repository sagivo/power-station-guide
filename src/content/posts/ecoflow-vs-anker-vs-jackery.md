The big three — EcoFlow, Anker SOLIX, Jackery — plus BLUETTI and DJI cover ~90% of the US portable power station market. But you're not just buying a box; you're buying into an ecosystem of batteries, panels, apps and support. Here's how the philosophies differ.

## EcoFlow: the tech enthusiast's brand

**Strengths:** fastest charging in the industry (X-Stream AC, huge solar MPPTs), the best app (automations, storm alerts, time-of-use modes), and the widest range from 256Wh to 4kWh flagships.

**Flagships:** [DELTA Pro 3](amazon:ecoflow-delta-pro-3) (4096Wh/4000W), [DELTA 2 Max](amazon:ecoflow-delta-2-max) (2048Wh/2400W), [DELTA 3 Plus](amazon:ecoflow-delta-3-plus) (1024Wh/2400W).

**Weaknesses:** premium pricing (wait for sales), and the product line turns over fast — today's flagship is next year's mid-range.

**Buy EcoFlow if:** you want the smartest software, the fastest recharge, and the deepest bench of models.

## Anker SOLIX: the value disruptor

**Strengths:** the most aggressive pricing (flagship watts at mid-range sale prices), the only 6000W split-phase portable ([F3800](amazon:anker-solix-f3800)), and strong cycle ratings (4000 on newer models).

**Flagships:** [SOLIX F3800](amazon:anker-solix-f3800) (3840Wh/6000W), [F3000](amazon:anker-solix-f3000) (3072Wh/3600W), [C2000 Gen 2](amazon:anker-solix-c2000-gen2) (2048Wh/2400W), [C1000](amazon:anker-solix-c1000) (1056Wh/1800W).

**Weaknesses:** the app is good, not great; list prices are fictional (never pay MSRP); solar input caps are lower on the small models.

**Buy Anker if:** you want the most watts and watt-hours per dollar and don't mind waiting for a sale.

## Jackery: the beginner's brand

**Strengths:** the simplest experience (unbox, plug, done), the deepest accessory ecosystem (SolarSaga panels everywhere), whisper-quiet operation (~30 dB), and the most modular expansion story ([2000 Plus](amazon:jackery-explorer-2000-plus): 2→24kWh).

**Flagships:** [Explorer 2000 Plus](amazon:jackery-explorer-2000-plus) (2043Wh/3000W), [Explorer 1000 Plus](amazon:jackery-explorer-1000-plus) (1264Wh/2000W).

**Weaknesses:** slowest solar recharge of the three, premium pricing for the specs, and the app is thin.

**Buy Jackery if:** you want the easiest ownership experience and a one-brand accessory shelf.

## The verdict

There is no best brand — there's a best brand *for you*:

- **Maximum tech and speed:** EcoFlow
- **Maximum value:** Anker SOLIX
- **Maximum simplicity:** Jackery
- **Maximum battery lifespan:** BLUETTI ([Elite 200 V2](amazon:bluetti-elite-200-v2))
- **Maximum USB-C/drone integration:** DJI ([Power 1000](amazon:dji-power-1000))

Cross-brand compatibility is fine for the things that matter — any brand's MC4 solar panels work with any brand's station within voltage limits — so don't let ecosystem lock-in drive the decision. Buy the best *box* for your loads.
