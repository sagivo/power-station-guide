A few years ago, "budget power station" meant a heavy NMC box with a weak inverter. In 2026, $400–$700 buys LiFePO4 chemistry, 1800W+ of pure-sine output and recharge times under an hour. Here's the short list.

## Best budget overall: [Anker SOLIX C1000](amazon:anker-solix-c1000)

1056Wh, 1800W (2400W SurgePad), 58-minute full recharge, expandable to 2112Wh — frequently $400–$500 on sale. It does 90% of what most people need a station for: fridge for most of a day, all devices charged, campsite kitchen covered. [Full review](/power-stations/anker-solix-c1000/).

## Most powerful: [EcoFlow DELTA 3 Plus](amazon:ecoflow-delta-3-plus)

1024Wh with a **2400W** inverter — the most powerful 1kWh box here — plus expansion to 5.1kWh and a 10ms UPS. Around $800–$999. Buy it when your loads are spiky (tools, microwave) rather than long. [Full review](/power-stations/ecoflow-delta-3-plus/).

## Best for creators: [DJI Power 1000](amazon:dji-power-1000)

1024Wh, 2200W, dual 140W USB-C and SDC drone fast-charging at 23–26 dB — often $699 direct from DJI. The pick if your "loads" are cameras, laptops and drones. [Full review](/power-stations/dji-power-1000/).

## Best for the Jackery faithful: [Jackery Explorer 1000 Plus](amazon:jackery-explorer-1000-plus)

1264Wh, 2000W, expansion to 5kWh, ~$900–$1,199. Buy it for the ecosystem and simplicity; buy the Anker for the specs-per-dollar. [Full review](/power-stations/jackery-explorer-1000-plus/).
