The Anker SOLIX C1000 is the gateway power station: 1056Wh, a real 1800W inverter, and a 58-minute full recharge — frequently on sale under $500, which makes it the cheapest credible way into the category.

## Who the C1000 is for (and who should skip it)

Buy it as a first station for camping, tailgating and short outages. It runs a fridge for most of a day, charges everything you own, and recharges faster than anything near its price.

Skip it if you need genuine home backup — 1056Wh is one fridge-day. That's [2kWh territory](/power-stations/anker-solix-c2000-gen2/), not 1kWh.

## The 58-minute recharge

This is the C1000's superpower: 1300W AC input fills the battery in under an hour. Forgot to charge before the trip? Plug it in while you pack. No other budget station recharges this fast.

## Output

1800W continuous (2400W SurgePad for resistive loads) runs microwaves, coffee makers, electric griddles and most power tools — far beyond what 1kWh boxes offered a few years ago. Eleven ports including 100W USB-C.

## How it compares

Versus the [EcoFlow DELTA 3 Plus](/power-stations/ecoflow-delta-3-plus/): EcoFlow gives you 2400W and better expansion for roughly double the sale price; Anker wins on value. Versus the [DJI Power 1000](/power-stations/dji-power-1000/): DJI wins for creators (dual 140W USB-C); Anker wins on price, recharge speed and expandability.
