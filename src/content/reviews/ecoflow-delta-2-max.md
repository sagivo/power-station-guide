The EcoFlow DELTA 2 Max is the default answer in the 2kWh class: 2048Wh, 2400W of output, 50 lb — the lightest 2kWh station we track — with EcoFlow's best-in-class app and an expansion path to 6.1kWh.

## Who the DELTA 2 Max is for (and who should skip it)

Buy it if you want the safe mainstream pick: enough capacity for a full day of essentials, enough output for anything short of 240V, light enough to actually carry to the campsite.

Skip it if solar speed is the priority — 1000W combined input trails the [AC200L](/power-stations/bluetti-ac200l/) (1200W) and [F3000](/power-stations/anker-solix-f3000/) (2400W). Skip it if you want the absolute lowest price; the [Anker C2000 Gen 2](/power-stations/anker-solix-c2000-gen2/) undercuts it on sale.

## Output and surge

2400W continuous with a 4800W surge rating is generous — it starts fridge compressors and runs microwaves, kettles, and most job-site tools simultaneously. Six AC outlets is the most in this class.

## The EcoFlow app advantage

Scheduling, automations, storm alerts, time-of-use modes — EcoFlow's software is meaningfully ahead of BLUETTI and Jackery. If you plan to use the station daily (not just for outages), the app is a real reason to pick this platform.

## How it compares

Versus the [Anker C2000 Gen 2](/power-stations/anker-solix-c2000-gen2/): Anker is lighter, cheaper on sale and rated for more cycles; EcoFlow has more outlets and better software. Versus the [Elite 200 V2](/power-stations/bluetti-elite-200-v2/): BLUETTI wins on battery longevity; EcoFlow wins on expansion and weight.
