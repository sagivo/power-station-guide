The Anker SOLIX F3000 is what the mid-range should look like in 2026: 3072Wh, 3600W of output, and 2400W of solar input — the fastest solar recharge in the 3kWh class — in a 55 lb box that undercuts older flagships on price.

## Who the F3000 is for (and who should skip it)

Buy it if 2kWh isn't quite enough but 4kWh flagships are overkill. The 3kWh capacity hits the sweet spot for a full day-plus of real household loads, and the 2400W solar input means a big array actually pays off.

Skip it if you need 240V — that's the [F3800](/power-stations/anker-solix-f3800/). Note it's a newer platform, so long-term reliability data is thinner than the F3800's.

## Solar: the standout spec

2400W of MPPT is flagship-grade solar on a mid-range box. With a 2000W+ array in good sun, you're refilling most of the 3kWh in a single afternoon — the use case that makes rolling multi-day outages survivable without a generator.

## Capacity and output

3072Wh runs a modern fridge (~1.2kWh/day) plus freezer, lights, modem and devices for 36+ hours. 3600W continuous handles microwaves, kettles, sump pumps and job-site tools; surge covers motor startups.

## How it compares

Versus the [DELTA Pro 3](/power-stations/ecoflow-delta-pro-3/): Anker gives you 75% of the capacity with 92% of the solar speed for much less money and 60 fewer pounds. Versus the [Jackery 2000 Plus](/power-stations/jackery-explorer-2000-plus/): Anker wins on capacity, output and solar; Jackery is quieter and has the deeper expansion ceiling.
