The EcoFlow DELTA 3 Plus is for buyers who need watts more than watt-hours: a 2400W inverter — the most powerful in the 1kWh class — in a 28 lb box, with an expansion path to 5.1kWh that no rival under $1000 matches.

## Who the DELTA 3 Plus is for (and who should skip it)

Buy it if your loads are spiky: power tools, kettles, microwaves — things that need a big inverter for short bursts. The 56-minute recharge and 10ms UPS make it a great workshop and home-office companion.

Skip it if you mostly need capacity for the money — the [Anker C1000](/power-stations/anker-solix-c1000/) gives you the same 1kWh for roughly half the sale price. The 500W solar cap is also the weakest here.

## The inverter is the product

2400W from a 1kWh box is unusual — most rivals this size top out at 1800–2000W. If you've ever tripped a smaller station running a microwave, this is the fix. The 4800W surge handles motor startups with ease.

## Expansion: 1kWh today, 5kWh later

This is the clever bit: start with 1024Wh, add DELTA 3 extra batteries up to 5120Wh total. No other budget station grows this far, which makes the 3 Plus the rare 1kWh unit you won't outgrow.

## How it compares

Versus the [Anker C1000](/power-stations/anker-solix-c1000/): EcoFlow wins on output and expansion; Anker wins on price and recharge speed. Versus the [DJI Power 1000](/power-stations/dji-power-1000/): EcoFlow wins on AC output and expandability; DJI wins on USB-C power and silence.
