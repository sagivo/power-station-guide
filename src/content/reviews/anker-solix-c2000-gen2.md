The Anker SOLIX C2000 Gen 2 wins the 2kWh value argument: 2048Wh, 2400W of output, a 4000-cycle battery and 10ms UPS in the lightest chassis in its class (41.7 lb) — frequently around $900 on sale.

## Who the C2000 Gen 2 is for (and who should skip it)

Buy it if you want the most 2kWh for the least money without buying junk. It's our homepage top pick for exactly this reason: the spec sheet embarrasses units costing 50% more.

Skip it if you're building a big solar array — the 800W solar cap wastes anything bigger. The [AC200L](/power-stations/bluetti-ac200l/) (1200W) or [F3000](/power-stations/anker-solix-f3000/) (2400W) are the solar-first alternatives.

## The value math

At ~$900 street, you're paying about $0.44/Wh — flagship territory is $0.70–0.90/Wh. The 4000-cycle rating means the battery should outlast the 3000-cycle competition by years under regular use.

## Output and UPS

2400W continuous covers microwaves, kettles and power tools; the 10ms UPS switchover is proper uninterruptible-supply territory for PCs, modems and CPAP machines. Five AC outlets plus fast USB-C round out a complete port selection.

## How it compares

Versus the [DELTA 2 Max](/power-stations/ecoflow-delta-2-max/): Anker is cheaper, lighter and longer-rated; EcoFlow has the better app and more outlets. Versus the [Elite 200 V2](/power-stations/bluetti-elite-200-v2/): BLUETTI's 6000-cycle battery wins for daily cycling; Anker wins on price and weight. This is the unit our [RV sizer](/tools/rv-sizer/) recommends most often.
